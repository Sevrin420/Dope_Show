DOPE SHOW

You owe the Loan Shark. You have 30 days. Run the wheel, buy low, sell high — and don't get caught.

  • Move: WASD / arrows (or hold the mouse / finger toward where you want to go). You can only run on the roads.
  • Towers: stop on a tower and press E to trade. The center tower also has the Loan Shark and a medic.
  • Buyers ($) wander the roads and pay a premium for the drug they want — run into them.
  • Cops ▲ patrol and confiscate stock. Gangs ▲ chase you and rob you.
  • Dash: Space gives a short burst of speed and makes you untouchable. It recharges.
  • Debt grows 8% a day. Final score = cash − debt.
